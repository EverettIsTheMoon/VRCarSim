LEFT-HAND RACER

VR (left hand): reach out and squeeze grip near the wheel to grab it, then move your hand around the wheel to turn it - your hand stays locked on the rim until you let go (the wheel re-centers on release) · grab the lever beside the wheel and push it forward = Drive, pull back = Reverse · trigger = gas · stick back = brake · stick click = recenter · X = get out/in of car (stick walks) · Y = repair (hold 1s = menu) · grab the handbrake on the right console and pull back (Space on keyboard) · hand tracking: pinch index = gas, pinch middle = grab, pinch ring = brake
Garage (inside gas station): touch a panel to change paint, wheel, engine, spoiler · drive-thru: stop at the McDonald's window, grab the fries and bring them to your mouth (H on keyboard) to eat · On foot: squeeze grip near fries/candy/drinks to grab, release to throw, swing a fist at a worker to punch · park by the gas pump to refuel · out of fuel? use the teleport buttons (keys 1 = gas station, 2 = McDonald's)
Keyboard: F grab/throw · G punch · W gas · S brake · A/D steer · R reverse lever · X repair · E get out/in
