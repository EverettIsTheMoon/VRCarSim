LEFT-HAND RACER

Driving (VR): grab the wheel (grip near it) and rotate your hand around it · lever beside the wheel: push forward = Drive, back = Reverse · trigger = gas · stick back = brake · handbrake on the right console (pull back) · red button = boost · Y = repair (hold 1s = menu) · X = get out / in
On foot: stick = walk · other stick = snap turn · X near car = get in, X away from car = pistol out/away · trigger = shoot · grip = pick up / throw items (gas station counter items can only be thrown, at the worker) · Y near bed = sleep (day/night) · Y near TV = TV on/off
Hand tracking: make a fist OR pinch middle finger = grab · pinch index = gas / shoot · pinch ring = brake · pinch pinky = X
Keyboard: W gas / walk · S brake · A/D steer (turn on foot) · R reverse lever · X repair · E car/pistol · F shoot · G sleep/TV · Space handbrake · B boost
