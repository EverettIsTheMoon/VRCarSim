VR Object Studio

A mixed-reality sandbox: supported Quest browsers show your real room using system passthrough. Press X on your left controller to open the object menu, point at a shape and press the trigger to spawn it. Hold Grip to move objects around your room. This does not read raw camera images.

Desktop preview also works: click to look around, use the on-screen menu, and click an object to select it.

Open on your Quest browser over HTTPS and choose Enter Mixed Reality. Passthrough is provided by Quest; this webpage does not access camera video.

X: menu · Modes: Place / Move / Resize / Throw · Grip: move
