⛽ Night Shift

Run a tiny late-night gas station in hand-tracked VR. Grab snacks and drinks by pinching your thumb and index finger together. Put the customer's requested item on the counter to serve them. A harmless foam blaster is tucked beside the register for arcade-style target practice.

Quest / WebXR: open in the headset browser, allow hand tracking, then enter VR. Desktop fallback: click items to grab/drop, click the blaster to fire, and use the buttons below.

Best in a WebXR-compatible browser over HTTPS. Hand tracking depends on headset/browser support.
