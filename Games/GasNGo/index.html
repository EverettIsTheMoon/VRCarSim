⛽ GAS-N-GO

Pinch to grab. Hand customers what's in their bubble. Hold food to your mouth to eat it. Throw money if you feel like it. To move: close your middle, ring and pinky fingers, point your index finger at the floor, and let go to teleport. Pinch the toy gun to pick it up, then make a fist to shoot.

Quest 2: turn on Hand Tracking in Settings → Movement tracking.
