OUTPOST FIRE

Bomb defusal: Terrorists carry the bomb to the site and plant it, Counter-Terrorists stop them or defuse it. First to 8 rounds; sides switch after round 7.
VR: trigger = fire, thumbstick = move, face button = reload, hold grip = defuse. Bring the rifle scope up to your eye to zoom.
Desktop: WASD move, mouse aim, click fire, right-mouse scope, R reload, hold E = plant (T) or defuse (CT).
Phone (landscape): drag left to move, right to aim, buttons for fire, scope, reload, use (defuse).
